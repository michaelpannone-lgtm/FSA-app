Interactive prototype

HSA Receipts

This runs the app’s real logic in the browser: the receipt reader, the eligibility rules, the split math and the reimbursement planner. The receipts are made-up examples.

  • Tap + and paste any receipt text to see how it gets read and tagged.
  • Open the Costco receipt and try Quick split.
  • On Reimburse, enter an amount and tap Find best combination.
  • Export a claim packet, then mark it reimbursed and watch the dashboard update.

The camera, the iCloud folder and the share sheet only exist on the iPhone, so here they’re stand-ins. Your changes are kept in this browser only.
